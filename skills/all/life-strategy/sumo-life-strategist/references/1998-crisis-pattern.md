# 1998 Crisis Pattern — How New Billionaires Were Built

## The Pattern
Krisis 1998 menghancurkan 80% bisnis Indonesia — tapi MENCIPTAKAN generasi triliuner baru.

| Phase | What They Did | Why It Worked |
|---|---|---|
| 1997-1998 (Peak Crisis) | Bought foreclosed assets at 20-30 cents on dollar | Assets dirt cheap because everyone was liquidating |
| 1998-2000 (Low Point) | Built consumer goods companies (local production) | Import stopped, domestic demand still there |
| 2000-2003 (Recovery) | Invested in properties (land, buildings) | Properties bottomed, banks willing to lend again |
| 2003-2008 (Boom) | Rode infrastructure boom, IPO'd companies | Super cycle in commodities + domestic consumption |

## Key Insight
"They didn't wait for the crisis to end. They built DURING the crisis."

Businesses that made the most money in 2008-2015 were started in 1999-2002.
