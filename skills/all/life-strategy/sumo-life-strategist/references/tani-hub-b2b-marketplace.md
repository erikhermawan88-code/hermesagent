# TANI HUB — B2B Agricultural Marketplace

## Vision
Platform B2B yang connect farmer langsung ke bisnis pembeli — tanpa middleman.

## The Problem
```
SEKARANG (Inefficient):
Petani → Kolektor → Gudang → Distributor → Pabrik/Restoran
          ↓            ↓           ↓            ↓
      Mark-up 20%   Mark-up 15%  Mark-up 20%  Mark-up 30%
      
TARGET (Our Platform):
Petani → TANI HUB → Bisnis Pembeli
         ↓
      Mark-up 5-10%
      
RESULT: Petani dapat harga 15-25% lebih tinggi, Pembeli hemat 15-20%
```

## Business Model
- **Commission:** 5-10% per transaction (varies by crop type)
- **Logistics Fee:** Rp 500-2000/km/ton (optional, platform arranged)
- **Premium Listing:** Rp 500-2000/bulan (featured farmers)
- **Financing Spread:** 2-3% (buyer pays upfront → farmer gets advance)

## Core Features
1. **Farmer Side:** Inventory upload, quality grading, order management, wallet/withdrawal
2. **Buyer Side:** Catalog browse, bulk order, recurring order, dashboard analytics
3. **Platform:** AI matching, escrow payment, logistics tracking, dispute resolution
4. **AI Layer:** Smart matching, price intelligence, demand forecasting, quality prediction

## Target Users
- **Sellers:** Individual farmers, kelompok tani, small farms (0.5-10 ha)
- **Buyers:** Restaurants, food factories, hotels, retailers, exporters

## MVP Scope (Month 1-3)
- WhatsApp-first OR simple web app
- 50 verified farmers + 10 verified buyers
- First 100 transactions
- Working escrow (Midtrans)
- Basic matching (manual or simple algorithm)

## 1998 Pattern Connection
- Disrupt agricultural middlemen (kolektor)
- Build direct farmer-to-buyer platform
- Farmer gets higher price, buyer gets lower cost
- Platform takes small cut but at massive volume
