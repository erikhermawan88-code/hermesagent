# TANI HUB — B2B Agricultural Marketplace

## Vision
Platform B2B yang connect farmer langsung ke bisnis pembeli — tanpa middleman.

## Core Features
- Farmer inventory upload → Business buyer browse → Order → Escrow payment → Logistics → Delivery → Rating

## Revenue
- Commission 5-10% per transaction
- Logistics fee Rp 500-2000/km/ton
- Premium listing Rp 500-2000/month

## Target
- 2026: 500 farmers, 50 buyers, Rp 500M GMV/month
- 2031: 80,000 farmers, 8,000 buyers, Rp 150B GMV/month
